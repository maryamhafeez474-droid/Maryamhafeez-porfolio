Why Medicine?

“I want to become the kind of doctor who can look at a person who is suffering and say: I can help.”

My motivation comes from seeing people who are sick, disabled, or unable to live their lives comfortably. I want to develop the knowledge, judgement and clinical skills needed to care for them properly.

I am especially interested in working with children because I have always loved being around them. I hope to become a doctor who combines strong medical ability with compassion and patience.

Why the United Kingdom?

The UK has been a long-standing study goal for me. I am drawn to the opportunity to learn within a healthcare environment known for structured medical education and clinical training.

My aim is to become a highly capable doctor through rigorous education and practical experience. My sister also lives in the UK, which would give me an important source of emotional support while I adjust to studying abroad.

The Doctor I Hope to Become

In five to ten years, I see myself becoming a knowledgeable, skilled and compassionate doctor — someone capable of treating patients confidently and helping them move towards healthier, happier lives.

I am particularly interested in caring for children, while remaining open to learning broadly throughout my medical education. I want every stage of my training to make me more capable of serving the people who place their trust in me.

Giving Back

One of my long-term goals is to use the education and experience I gain abroad to help people in Pakistan who cannot afford to travel overseas for treatment.

I hope to return with strong medical skills and contribute to better access to care for patients who might otherwise have limited options. I want my education to create value beyond my own career — especially for people who need medical help but have fewer resources.

Why This Scholarship Matters

Studying MBBS in the UK is a serious financial challenge for my family. The tuition cost is the main barrier between me and a goal I have worked towards for years.

This scholarship would not simply help me enter a university. It would give me the opportunity to pursue the medical education I have dreamed about, develop the skills I need to become a doctor, and eventually use those skills to serve others.

I would approach that opportunity with determination, curiosity and a genuine commitment to medicine.
